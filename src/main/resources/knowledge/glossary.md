# Glossary (English / French)

## COD (cash on delivery) / paiement a la livraison
The customer pays the courier in cash at the door. The courier later remits the cash to the merchant,
minus its fees.

## Remittance / versement
The money the courier pays back to the merchant. Net remitted = collected - fees.

## Statement / releve
The courier's periodic CSV file listing delivered parcels, collected amounts and fees.

## Reconciliation / rapprochement
Matching every statement line against the merchant's orders to find discrepancies (ecarts).

## Discrepancy / ecart
Any non-matched line: amount mismatch (ecart de montant), unknown tracking number (numero de suivi
inconnu) or parcel missing from the statement (colis manquant sur le releve).

## Dispute / litige
A claim the merchant opens against a discrepancy, resolved or rejected by finance operations.

## Balance owed / solde du
Cash the courier collected (or should have collected) but has not reported yet.

## Fee / frais
What the courier keeps per parcel, for delivery or for a return.

## Courier / livreur, transporteur
The delivery company, for example Speedex Colis or Rapido Express.
